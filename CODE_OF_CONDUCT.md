# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics,
gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful of differing viewpoints, experience levels, and technical judgment.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the project and the people using it.
- Show empathy toward other community members.

Because this project is a measurement framework, one further norm matters: **disagreement about
numbers is resolved with evidence, not volume.** If you believe a weight, formula, or threshold
is wrong, bring a trace, a test, or a calibration result. "This score feels wrong" is a fine way
to open a discussion and a poor way to close one.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention or advances.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address, without
  explicit permission.
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions,
and code review — and in public spaces when an individual is representing the project.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the
project maintainers through a
[private security advisory](https://github.com/RoofTopDude/pau-profiler/security/advisories/new),
which is the most reliable private channel available on this repository.

All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. Maintainers are obligated
to respect the privacy and security of the reporter of any incident.

Maintainers who do not follow or enforce this Code of Conduct in good faith may face temporary
or permanent repercussions as determined by other members of the project's leadership.
